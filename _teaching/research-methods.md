Research Methods is where students begin to conduct psychological research themselves. They learn to develop a question, evaluate prior work, choose a design, collect and analyze data, and explain their findings. I have taught the material both as a one-semester course and as a two-course sequence.

## What Students Study

We cover research ethics, measurement, sampling, descriptive and correlational research, experimental design, validity, and statistical inference. Students learn to use statistical software and communicate methods and results in APA style.

## What Students Do

- **Complete a collaborative correlational study:** Teams develop a survey, define variables, collect data, and write up their analyses.
- **Design an experiment:** Students work from a theory-based question through hypotheses, materials, conditions, ethical considerations, and an analysis plan. In versions with a full experimental component, students collect and analyze data after required approvals.
- **Communicate findings:** Students prepare APA-style reports and research posters. Some projects develop into conference presentations or publications.

I emphasize the decisions researchers make along the way, including what can reasonably be concluded from the data. Students use accessible statistical tools alongside open educational resources.
