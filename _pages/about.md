I'm Keith Wylie, an Assistant Professor of Psychology at Flagler College in St. Augustine, Florida. I earned my Ph.D. in Legal Psychology at Florida International University and previously taught at Emporia State University.

My research examines how people remember, interpret, and communicate information in legal settings. I'm especially interested in eyewitness memory and investigative interviewing, the language people use to describe legal events, and the decisions made by jurors and other legal actors. Recent projects also explore misinformation, beliefs about criminality, and how people evaluate evidence.

I teach legal and forensic psychology, research methods, psychology of language, and other courses in psychology and Flagler's Core curriculum. In my classes, students work with original research, real-world cases, and hands-on activities. I also mentor undergraduate researchers as they develop studies and share their findings.

You can explore my [teaching and course materials](/teaching/), browse my [publications](/publications/), or see my [conference presentations](/presentations/).
